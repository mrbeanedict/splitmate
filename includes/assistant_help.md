## What Setlo is
Setlo splits shared food bills fairly. You scan the receipt, assign each item to the people who ordered it, and Setlo works out who pays whom. A payment only counts as settled after the person receiving the money confirms it.

## How to split a bill (tutorial)
1. Tap New Bill on [My bills](my-bills), name it and add the people (or pick a saved group).
2. Open [Scan a receipt](scan-receipt) for that bill. Take a photo or upload one. Setlo reads the items and prices. A long receipt can be photographed in parts, and one bill can have several receipts ("Add another receipt").
3. Review the items: fix any misread name, quantity or price, then Continue. Setlo may ask whether to round the total to whole pesos, and who received the change if several people paid more than the bill.
4. Assign items: tap an item, then the people who ordered it. Shared charges (tax, service charge) are split for you. The "unassigned" amount must reach zero before you can settle.
5. Bill detail shows each person's share and the settlement plan (who pays whom). Start settling to lock it in.
6. Everyone pays outside the app (cash, bank transfer, GCash, Maya, card) or online with PayMongo (pesos only), then taps Mark paid on [Settle up](my-settlements).
7. The person receiving the money taps Confirm (it becomes Settled) or Report (it becomes Disputed). When every settlement is settled, the bill closes.

## Scanning tips
- Good light, flat surface, whole receipt in frame. Blurry or dark photos may fail; try again or use Enter manually.
- If the photo isn't a receipt, Setlo says so and nothing is saved.
- Scanning needs internet and uses Google Gemini, which can be busy; wait a minute and retry.
- Photos of the same receipt twice are flagged as duplicates.

## Settling and paying
- Mark paid: the sender says they paid. Confirm: the receiver agrees. Nothing is settled until the receiver confirms.
- You can pay in parts (several methods) and someone can pay on your behalf. Installment interest may be added if the creator set it.
- A nudge reminds someone who owes you (there is a short cooldown between nudges).
- Settlement audit trail shows when each step happened.
- Your Pay-me QR (Profile) opens a page with your payment details; "Get a new QR" disables the old one.

## Currencies
- Your home currency (Profile) is what the dashboard and stats add up in. Amounts in other currencies are converted at today's market rate and shown with ≈.
- A bill settles in one agreed currency (starts as the payer's; the creator can change it on Bill detail until settling starts).
- The scanner detects the receipt's currency (for example dollars). On the Review page you can correct it. Each line shows ≈ its value in the settlement currency.
- Bill detail: rate options are the market estimate, the actual amount charged (best for foreign receipts) or a rate you type, plus fees and rounding.
- PayMongo online payment is pesos only.

## Groups and Fun Mode
- Save a group for faster bills. The group's creator can switch on Fun Mode: after the receipt review, the group can play a game to decide the split: Receipt Race (fastest correct total pays nothing), Closest Without Going Over, Roulette (last one standing pays all), Mystery Card (cards change your share). "Split normally" is always available.

## Achievements
One-time badges (Itemalizer, Kuripot, Glutton, Samaritan, Debt Collector, Clean Slate and more) are earned from using Setlo; open your profile to see them and choose a title.

## Account
- Log in with email and password or Continue with Google. Forgot password sends a 6-digit code by email (valid 10 minutes).
- After 5 wrong passwords, sign-in is locked for 15 minutes.
- Theme: Profile → accent colour and light/dark/system.

## Limits
- Setlo tracks and verifies payments; it does not move money.
- Receipt scanning accuracy varies; always check the items.
- Exchange rates are estimates updated about daily.
